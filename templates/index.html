Research Overview
METHODOLOGY AND SCOPE
Objective
WHAT THIS STUDY DOES

Quantify the rate of household expenditure change across income brackets using the Central Difference Formula, and compute cumulative expenditure burden per region using the Trapezoidal Rule - applied to the 2015 PSA FIES dataset.

Dataset
DATA SOURCE

2015 PSA Family Income and Expenditure Survey - 41,544 household records, 60 variables, 17 administrative regions. Sourced from the Philippine Statistics Authority and verified by the research team.

Novel Contribution
WHAT'S NEW

First application of numerical differentiation and integration methods to Philippine FIES data - producing rate-based insights no prior descriptive study has generated on this dataset.

About the Study
RESEARCH BACKGROUND AND DOCUMENTATION

Research Title

Quantifying the Rate and Cumulative Burden of Household Expenditure Change Across Philippine Regions: A Numerical Methods Approach to the 2015 PSA FIES Dataset

Course & Institution

CS ELEC 01 - Computational Science for Computer Science

University of Southern Mindanao
College of Information and Computing Sciences
Academic Year 2024–2025

Methodology

Central Difference Formula - measures how fast expenditure changes per ₱1 income increase at each income bracket.

Trapezoidal Rule - computes cumulative expenditure burden accumulated across all income levels per region.

Both methods are implemented in Python and served via a Flask REST API.
