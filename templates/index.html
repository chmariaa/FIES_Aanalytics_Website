Research Overview
METHODOLOGY AND SCOPE
Objective
WHAT THIS STUDY DOES

Determine the rate of change in household expenditures among different income classes using the Central Difference Formula, and calculate the cumulative burden of expenses by region using the Trapezoidal Rule based on the 2015 PSA FIES data set.

Dataset
DATA SOURCE

PSA Family Income and Expenditure Survey 2015 - Total records: 41,544; variables: 60; geographical units: 17. Extracted from the Philippine Statistics Authority and validated by the research team.

Novel Contribution
WHAT'S NEW

First use of numerical differentiation and integration techniques in Philippine FIES data - yielding derivative-based analysis never achieved by any previous descriptive analysis using this dataset.

About the Study
RESEARCH BACKGROUND AND DOCUMENTATION

Research Title

Quantifying the Rate and Cumulative Burden of Household Expenditure Change Across Philippine Regions: A Numerical Methods Approach to the 2015 PSA FIES Dataset

Course & Institution

CS ELEC 01 - Computational Science for Computer Science

University of Southern Mindanao
College of Information and Computing Sciences
Academic Year 2024–2025

Methodology

Central Difference Formula - measures how fast expenditure changes per ₱1 income increase at each income bracket.

Trapezoidal Rule - computes cumulative expenditure burden accumulated across all income levels per region.

Both methods are implemented in Python and served via a Flask REST API.
